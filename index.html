About me

Embedded Systems Engineer with a passion for building intelligent electronic products and Industrial IoT solutions. I enjoy transforming ideas into reliable, real-world devices by combining embedded software, hardware design, and modern communication technologies.

My work focuses on developing robust embedded systems, designing custom electronic hardware, and creating connected products that solve real engineering challenges. From circuit design and firmware development to industrial communication and cloud integration, I enjoy taking projects from concept to deployment.

Currently, I'm expanding my expertise in advanced embedded architectures, Industrial IoT, RTOS, and modern product development, with the goal of building scalable and innovative technologies for the next generation of connected systems.

What i'm doing

  • Embedded Hardware Design

    Designing reliable electronic circuits, selecting components, and developing custom PCBs for industrial electronics, IoT devices, and smart products.

  • Electronic Product Development

    Transforming ideas into production-ready electronic products, from concept, prototyping, and hardware integration to testing and deployment.

  • Industrial IoT Solutions

    Developing reliable Industrial IoT systems for factories, commercial buildings, and infrastructure using ESP32, MQTT, Modbus, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, LoRa, BLE, and cloud platforms for monitoring, automation, and remote management.

  • Smart Home Automation

    Designing reliable wired and wireless smart home systems tailored to each client's needs. From lighting, climate control, and energy management to fully customized automation, dashboards, and user interfaces for complete home control.
